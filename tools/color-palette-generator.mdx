# Color Palette Generator

The Color Palette Generator is a powerful tool designed to help creators, designers, and developers quickly generate harmonious color schemes for their projects.

## Features

- Generate color palettes based on color theory principles
- Adjust hue, saturation, and brightness
- Export palettes in various formats (HEX, RGB, HSL)
- Save and share your favorite palettes

## How to Use

1. Choose a base color or start with a random palette
2. Adjust the harmony type (complementary, analogous, triadic, etc.)
3. Fine-tune individual colors as needed
4. Export your palette in your preferred format

## Why Use a Color Palette Generator?

Choosing the right colors for your project can be challenging. A well-designed color palette can:

- Enhance the visual appeal of your design
- Improve user experience and readability
- Convey the right mood and message for your brand

Our Color Palette Generator takes the guesswork out of color selection, allowing you to create beautiful, harmonious color schemes with ease.
