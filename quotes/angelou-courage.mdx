---
author: "Maya Angelou"
quote: "Courage is the most important of all the virtues because without courage, you can't practice any other virtue consistently."
---

Maya Angelou, a renowned American poet, memoirist, and civil rights activist, shared this powerful insight about the fundamental importance of courage.

This quote suggests that courage is the foundation upon which all other virtues are built. Without courage, one might falter in their commitment to honesty, compassion, integrity, or any other virtue when faced with adversity or fear.

Angelou's words remind us that living a virtuous life often requires us to step out of our comfort zones, face our fears, and stand up for what we believe in, even when it's difficult.
