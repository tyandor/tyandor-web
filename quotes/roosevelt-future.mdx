---
author: "Eleanor Roosevelt"
quote: "The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."
---

Eleanor Roosevelt, former First Lady of the United States and a prominent humanitarian, shared this inspiring quote about the power of belief and aspiration.

This quote encourages people to have faith in their dreams and aspirations. It suggests that those who can envision a better future and believe in their ability to create it are the ones who will shape the world to come.

Roosevelt's words remind us that progress and change often start with a vision - a dream of what could be. By believing in these dreams and working towards them, we can play an active role in creating the future we want to see.
