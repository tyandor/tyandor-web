Quoted in _Learning Systems Thinking_, Ch. 2.

It's interesting to encounter such a philosophical citation in a text oriented around software engineering, a call to picture our mental model of rationality itself.

This reminds me of Demming's system of profound knowledge, in which he identifies the study of human psychology as part of quality assurance. Similarly, it seems that knowledge workers in the context of complex systems might find useful insights in the study of logic, epistemology, and neuroscience.
