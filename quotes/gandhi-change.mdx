---
author: "Mahatma Gandhi"
quote: "Be the change you wish to see in the world."
---

This powerful quote is attributed to Mahatma Gandhi, the leader of India's non-violent independence movement against British rule and advocate for civil rights.

Gandhi's words encapsulate his philosophy of leading by example. The quote suggests that instead of waiting for others to make the world a better place, we should take the initiative to embody the changes we want to see.

This idea emphasizes personal responsibility and the potential for individual actions to have far-reaching effects. It encourages us to align our behavior with our ideals, recognizing that societal change often begins with personal transformation.
