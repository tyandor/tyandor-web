My daughter had just turned 4, and as we drove the winding streets of Colorado Springs she asked a question I can't recall. The follow up questions, though, have stuck with me. 

After I had answered the initial question, she came back with, "Are you sure?" 

"Pretty sure," I replied, partly because it reminds me of a lovely dialogue in one of my favorite stories.

"Why aren't you all the way sure?"

She asked this with such genuine curiosity, and it opened the door to a fun philosophical adventure.
 
This was my first experience of such an early verbal expression of the desire for certainty. While the conversation touched only on trivial matters, I find the idea of *certainty* thoroughly distasteful. It kills curiosity and stunts intellectual growth.
